About these tracks and comparisons

The all-cell scATAC tracks are CPM-normalized condition pseudobulks from the processed peak matrix. The nine selectable cell populations are fragment-derived pseudobulks reconstructed from the single-cell matrix and fragments in GSE153479 from Wang et al. (2020). Each selected population displays the four injury/age conditions on a shared scale.

For injury studies, P1 and P8 are the ages at surgery. PSD3 samples were collected at approximately P4 and P11, respectively. Optional matched H3K27ac tracks are individual biological replicates from GSE123867 and Wang et al. (2019).

P0 H3K27ac and WGBS are uninjured heart developmental references, not injury-matched controls. WGBS tracks show strand-specific CpG methylation percentages on a fixed 0–100% scale; a high value therefore represents more DNA methylation and should not be interpreted like a high accessibility or H3K27ac signal. All displayed data use the mm10/GRCm38 assembly.

The authors plotted cell-type and condition pseudobulks—not one browser track per cell. We reconstructed per-barcode labels with the reported peak-count and blacklist QC, TF-IDF/LSI/graph clustering, the positive marker peaks in Table S2, and the final per-condition cell counts in Table S1. Because a barcode-level TSS score was not deposited, low-confidence cells were excluded to those published totals. Independent held-out marker peaks were enriched in all nine reconstructed populations. The tracks preserve single-cell-derived population specificity while pooling cells to obtain interpretable browser coverage.

Important provenance: neither GEO nor the supplement contains the authors' original barcode-to-cell-type table, so these are independently re-derived labels and may not match their assignments barcode-for-barcode. See the annotation audit, barcode assignments, held-out validation, track-level marker validation, and track checksums.
